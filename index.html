These guides are intended to be used as checklists for things you find throughout the game.

There may be unintentional spoilers. Consider yourself warned.

These guides will not show you how to do something, or tell you specifically where it is on a map. There are other guides for that.

The intention is to use your phone, tablet, or laptop next to you while you play, so that you can quickly note the things you do as you do them.

Tapping any checkpoint will auto-save. The bottom of each page will tell you how long ago it was saved. There is a separate "Save" button if you are paranoid.

All progress is saved in your device's browser storage. If you:

  • change browsers
  • change devices
  • reinstall your browser
  • clear your browser data / cache
You may (likely) lose your progress!
